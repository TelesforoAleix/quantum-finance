# Methodology and Limitations

The project studies gate-based quantum computing in finance. It connects four
stages: framework synthesis, systematic identification and classification,
thematic synthesis, and experimental assessment.

The review framework distinguishes the financial problem, quantum method,
evaluation approach, and maturity of the evidence. Classification supports
comparisons within problem domains and synthesis across domains. The research
corpus contains 777 classified papers.

Assessment of practical advantage must state what is counted: data preparation
and loading, quantum computation, resource assumptions, and the classical
comparison. A resource estimate or an illustrative implementation is not by
itself a faithful replication of a paper or evidence of practical advantage.

## What Can Be Reproduced Here

The utility tests exercise text chunking, truncation, and structured logging on
synthetic inputs. They need neither the research corpus nor a model API.

This edition does not contain the full experimental pipeline, paper-level
evidence, screening decisions, or result tables. It therefore cannot independently
reproduce or validate the thesis findings. The earlier linked repositories
describe their own workflows and reflect earlier stages of the project.
